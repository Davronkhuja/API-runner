# Uchinchi tomon kutubxonalari (vendored)

Skript sandbox'ida (`apirunner/core/scripting/libs/`) o'zgarishsiz quyidagi kutubxonalar bor. Hammasi MIT litsenziyasi ostida. Versiyalari va fayllari:

| Kutubxona | Versiya | Fayl | Litsenziya |
|---|---|---|---|
| chai | 4.5.0 | `libs/chai.js` | MIT |
| lodash | 4.18.1 | `libs/lodash.min.js` | MIT |
| crypto-js | 4.2.0 | `libs/crypto-js.js` | MIT |
| dayjs | 1.11.23 | `libs/dayjs.min.js` | MIT |
| moment | 2.31.0 | `libs/moment.min.js` | MIT |

Yangilash: `npm i chai@4 lodash crypto-js dayjs moment` va yuqoridagi fayllarni nusxalash (`docs/adr/0007-script-sandbox.md`), so'ng ushbu jadvalni yangilang. Frontend (`web/package.json`) bog'liqliklari (Vue, CodeMirror, Inter, JetBrains Mono ...) o'z litsenziyalari bilan npm paketlarida; Inter va JetBrains Mono shriftlari SIL Open Font License 1.1.
